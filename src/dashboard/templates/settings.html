Template Variables Guide
Use these exact placeholders in your templates to inject live data:
{barn_temp} - Barn Temp (°C)
{barn_humidity} - Barn Humidity (%)
{barn_thi} - Temperature Humidity Index
{zone_temp} - Thermal Temp of the Pig (°C)
{duration} - Minutes the pig was stationary
{stationary_count} - Number of pigs down (Population Alert)
{total_count} - Total pigs detected (Population Alert)
